Omkar M. Kharade — Chartered Accountant

CA Omkar M. Kharade (B.Com., A.C.A.) is the founder and proprietor of Omkar Kharade & Co., Chartered Accountants, based in Virar (West), Palghar. He supports individuals, professionals and growing businesses with audit, taxation, GST, accounting and advisory work, and stays personally involved in every client matter.

Qualifications

  • Bachelor of Commerce (B.Com.)
  • Associate Chartered Accountant (A.C.A.), The Institute of Chartered Accountants of India

Areas of Practice

  • Statutory, tax and internal audit
  • Income tax return filing, tax planning and notices
  • GST registration, returns and reconciliation
  • Bookkeeping, final accounts and MIS reporting
  • Company law, ROC and LLP compliance
  • Business structuring and financial advisory

Approach to Client Work

Clients choose a CA for clarity, so Omkar explains every filing and decision in plain language. The focus is on accuracy, timely compliance and practical advice that fits the size and stage of your business. You deal directly with a qualified partner, not a call centre.

Who He Works With

Salaried taxpayers, doctors and other professionals, traders, small manufacturers, startups and partnership firms across Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar and Mumbai, with online support for clients elsewhere in India.

How a Typical Engagement Works

  • Discussion: a short conversation about your income, business and goals.
  • Document list: a clear checklist of what we need and by when.
  • Review and preparation: we check your records, prepare the return, accounts or filing, and flag risks.
  • Your approval: you review the figures and confirm before we file.
  • Filing and follow-up: we file, share the acknowledgement and track refunds, notices or later steps.

Omkar’s practice combines compliance work with advice.
