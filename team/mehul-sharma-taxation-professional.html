Mehul Sharma — Taxation Professional

Mehul Sharma is a taxation professional at Omkar Kharade & Co. He is a B.Com graduate from M. L. Dahanukar College and has cleared CA Final Group II. With more than five years of experience, he specialises in direct and indirect taxation for individuals and businesses.

Qualifications

  • B.Com, M. L. Dahanukar College
  • CA Final, Group II cleared

Expertise

  • Direct tax: income tax returns, assessments, scrutiny, appeals and litigation
  • Indirect tax: GST compliance and related matters
  • Regulatory compliance and notice management

Focus and Approach

Mehul's focus is client service and integrity. He prefers practical solutions that hold up under departmental review and give lasting value, instead of quick fixes. Clients dealing with an income tax notice or GST return queries benefit from his hands-on experience with assessments and appeals.

Income Tax Notices, Assessments and Appeals

Mehul Sharma works mainly on direct tax matters. If you receive an income tax notice, such as an intimation, a mismatch query, a scrutiny notice or a demand, he reviews the notice, checks it against your return, AIS and Form 26AS, and prepares a reply with the supporting documents. For larger matters he assists with assessment proceedings and appeals. Replying correctly and on time is important, so please do not ignore a notice. See our service page on income tax return filing for more detail.

GST Compliance and Queries

On the indirect tax side, Mehul supports GST return filing, reconciliation of sales and purchase data with GSTR-1, GSTR-3B and GSTR-2B, input tax credit checks, and replies to GST notices. These checks reduce errors that later lead to demands or blocked credit. Learn more on our GST registration and return filing page.

Who Benefits Most From His Support

  • Individuals and professionals who received an income tax notice or a refund query.
  • Traders and small businesses with regular GST returns and credit reconciliation.
  • Employers with TDS and payroll compliance questions.
  • Taxpayers with capital gains, rental income or income from several sources.

Working Style

Mehul prefers a practical, document-based approach.
