Forms Guide: ITR, GST & ROC

Choosing the wrong form causes defective returns and delays. This guide helps taxpayers in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar and Naigaon pick the right one.

Common Forms

  • ITR forms: different forms apply to salaried persons, business owners and professionals, depending on income type
  • Form 16 and Form 26AS: salary TDS certificate and tax credit statement
  • GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B: sales details and tax summary return
  • AOC-4 and MGT-7: annual financial statements and annual return for companies
  • DIR-3 KYC: director identity update

Income Tax Return Forms

The return form depends on who you are and what you earn. Different ITR forms exist for salaried individuals with simple income, individuals with capital gains or business income, firms and LLPs, companies, and trusts. Choosing the wrong one can make a return defective. Related forms include Form 16 from your employer, Form 26AS and the Annual Information Statement (AIS) for tax credit checks, Form 15G and 15H for lower TDS, and Form 10-IE to choose the new tax regime if you have business income. Our income tax return filing team confirms the right form before filing.

GST Forms and Returns

Common GST forms include GST REG-01 for registration, GSTR-1 for outward supplies, GSTR-3B for the monthly summary and tax payment, GSTR-9 for the annual return, and GSTR-9C for reconciliation where applicable. Composition dealers use a separate statement, and amendments or cancellation use their own forms. Every form must match your books and your GSTR-2B credit data. See our GST registration and return filing page for filing support.

Company and LLP Forms (MCA)

Companies file AOC-4 for financial statements and MGT-7 or MGT-7A for the annual return, while directors use DIR-3 KYC to keep their identity details current. Other forms cover appointment of auditors (ADT-1), director changes, share allotment and deposits. LLPs file Form 11 for the annual return and Form 8 for the statement of accounts and solvency. We handle these on the MCA portal as part of our company registration and ROC compliance service.

Tax Audit and TDS Forms

Tax audit reports are filed in Form 3CA or 3CB with the details in Form 3CD, and an audit is explained on our audit and assurance page. TDS statements are filed quarterly in Form 24Q for salary, Form 26Q for most other payments to residents, and Form 27Q for payments to non-residents, with Form 16 and Form 16A issued as certificates.

Why Documents and Forms Must Match

Tax departments compare your return with TDS data, GST data, bank information and MCA filings. A form filled with a figure that does not match these sources attracts notices. Keep your PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, invoices and TDS certificates ready before we begin.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which ITR form should I use?

It depends on your income sources and status. Share your details and we will tell you.

Can you fill these forms for me?

Yes, we prepare and file them after reviewing your documents.

Which ITR form should a salaried person with a small business use?

A person with business or professional income usually cannot use the simplest form. The right form depends on income sources and any presumptive taxation, so send us the details and we will confirm it.

What happens if I file the wrong form?

The return can be treated as defective and you may have to correct or refile it. It is cheaper to confirm the right form first.

What documents do you need to file my forms?

PAN, Aadhaar, bank statements, Form 16 or TDS certificates, AIS and Form 26AS, sales and purchase records for businesses, and any investment proofs.
