Tax Utilities & Official Portals

Most tax tasks happen on a government portal. This page lists the most-used official sites, with tips from our CA team serving Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar and Naigaon.

Key Official Portals

  • Income Tax e-filing: incometax.gov.in for returns, AIS, Form 26AS and notices
  • GST Portal: gst.gov.in for registration, returns and notices
  • MCA Portal: mca.gov.in for company and LLP filings
  • TRACES: for TDS statements and Form 16
  • CBIC: cbic.gov.in for customs and GST references

Safety Tips

Always type the official address yourself. Do not share OTPs, and be careful of messages claiming a refund. Government sites end in .gov.in.

What You Can Do on Each Portal

The Income Tax e-filing portal lets you file returns, view Form 26AS and AIS, respond to notices, check refund status, pay tax, and link PAN with Aadhaar. The GST portal handles registration, return filing, challans, refunds, notices and the GSTR-2B statement. The MCA portal is used for company and LLP incorporation, annual filings, director KYC and name or address changes. TRACES is where deductors file TDS statement corrections and download Form 16 and Form 16A. CBIC carries notifications and circulars for GST, customs and central excise.

Useful Tasks to Do Before You File

  • Check your AIS and Form 26AS for income and TDS you may have forgotten.
  • Confirm that your PAN is linked with Aadhaar and your bank account is pre-validated for refunds.
  • Keep your registered mobile number and email active, since OTPs and notices go there.
  • For GST, compare GSTR-2B with your purchase records before claiming credit.
  • For companies, make sure each director has an active DIN and has completed DIR-3 KYC.

Using the Portals Safely

Always type the web address yourself instead of tapping a link in a message. Do not share your login password or OTP with anyone who calls claiming to be a tax officer, bank employee or refund agent. If a notice arrives on email, log in to the portal separately to confirm that it appears in your account. Authorising a Chartered Accountant on the portal does not give away your password, and you can withdraw the authorisation later.

Common Portal Problems and Fixes

Typical problems include OTP not received because the mobile number is outdated, a PAN-Aadhaar link error, a failed payment that does not show in the challan history, a digital signature that will not register, and a notice that is not visible after login. Most of these are fixed by updating your profile details or waiting for processing and trying again. If the problem continues, send us a screenshot and we will tell you the next step. Frequently Asked Questions

Can you file for me on these portals?

Yes, with your authorisation we file returns and replies through the relevant portal.

Is a CA login needed for filing?

Taxpayers can file themselves, and a CA can file on their behalf after authorisation.

What should I do if I receive an income tax or GST notice on email?

Do not click links in the message. Log in to the official portal yourself, confirm that the notice is there, note the due date for the reply, and send it to us for review.

Can I authorise a CA on the income tax and GST portals?

Yes. You can authorise a Chartered Accountant to act for you on each portal, and you can withdraw the authorisation whenever you want.

Why is my refund not showing?

Common reasons are a processing delay, a mismatch in bank details, a PAN-Aadhaar issue or an outstanding demand. Share your acknowledgement and we will check the cause.
