Tax & Compliance Bulletins

Tax and compliance rules change often, and a missed notification can cost money. This Knowledge Bank section explains important updates in plain language for taxpayers and businesses in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar and Naigaon.

Where Official Updates Come From

  • Income Tax Department notifications and circulars
  • CBIC and GST Council notifications
  • Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) circulars

How to Use These Bulletins

Read the summary, check the effective date, and contact us if the update affects your return or business. Always confirm current rules before acting, as dates and limits can change.

Income Tax Updates Worth Tracking

The Income Tax Department regularly changes due dates, forms, TDS rules, and the way notices and refunds work. Salaried taxpayers should watch for changes to tax slabs, the old and new regime choice, standard deduction and TDS on salary, while business owners should watch for changes to tax audit, presumptive taxation and advance tax. We explain each update in plain language and show who is affected. For help applying an update to your own return, see our income tax return filing service.

GST Updates That Affect Your Returns

The GST Council meets often, and its decisions appear as notifications and circulars from CBIC and state tax departments. Typical updates cover rate changes, return due dates, e-invoicing limits, input tax credit conditions, and amnesty or relief schemes. If you file GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B every month, a single missed update on invoice reporting or credit rules can lead to notices later. Our GST registration and return filing team reviews each update against your own filings.

ROC and Company Law Updates

The Ministry of Corporate Affairs issues circulars on annual filing, director KYC, new forms, fee relief and compliance for small companies and LLPs. Changes here matter because missed filings carry additional fees every day. If you run a private limited company or LLP, our company registration and ROC compliance team keeps a filing calendar so you do not depend on memory.

A Simple Monthly Compliance Checklist

  • Every month: GST returns, TDS deposit and payroll related dues such as PF and ESI where applicable.
  • Every quarter: TDS returns, advance tax instalments for eligible taxpayers and QRMP related tasks for GST filers.
  • Every year: income tax return, tax audit where applicable, annual GST return, company or LLP annual filing and director KYC.

Dates vary by taxpayer type and are sometimes extended, so confirm the current due date on the official portal or with us before you rely on it.

How to Avoid Fake Tax Messages

Updates spread quickly on WhatsApp, and fake notices and refund links spread with them. Do not open links from unknown senders, do not share OTPs, and always check a notice by logging in to the official portal yourself. Our official portals page lists the genuine government sites.
