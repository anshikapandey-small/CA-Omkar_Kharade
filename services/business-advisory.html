Business Advisory & Financial Consulting Services

Growing a business needs more than compliance. It needs numbers you can trust and a plan. Our business advisory services cover startup setup, project reports for bank loans, cash flow planning and tax-efficient structuring for owners in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar, Naigaon and nearby areas.

How We Help

  • Startup guidance on structure, registrations and licences
  • Project reports and CMA data for bank and MUDRA loans
  • Budgeting, cash flow and cost analysis
  • Business valuation and partner exit support
  • Tax-efficient structuring and succession planning

Bank Loan Support

Lenders look at your past financials, projections and repayment capacity. We prepare a clear, realistic report and guide you on documents so your application is complete the first time.

Starting a Business in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai and Palghar

The first decisions are often the costly ones: proprietorship, partnership, LLP or private limited company, the right registrations, bank account, GST and licences. We compare the options on tax, compliance cost, liability and funding needs, and then help you complete the setup through our company registration and ROC compliance service. Early advice also helps you choose between the old and new tax regime and plan your first-year compliance.

Project Reports, CMA Data and MUDRA Loans

A bank wants to see where the money will be used, how the business will earn, and how the loan will be repaid. We prepare project reports, projected balance sheets and profit statements, cash-flow estimates and CMA data for term loans, working capital limits and MUDRA loans. Our reports use your past financials where available, which are maintained through our accounting and bookkeeping service. We also advise on the documents the bank will ask for, though the final approval always rests with the lender.

Udyam (MSME) Registration and Government Schemes

Micro, small and medium enterprises can register on the Udyam portal, which can help with priority-sector lending, subsidy schemes and payment protection. We help you register and advise whether any scheme suits your business. Eligibility and benefits vary, so we check the current conditions before you apply.

Budgeting, Cash-Flow Planning and Cost Control

Profit on paper does not always mean cash in the bank. We help owners plan monthly cash flow, set a realistic budget, track receivables and control costs. A simple monthly review of sales, margins and dues often shows where to cut cost or collect faster.

Tax-Efficient Structuring and Succession

Choosing the right structure can reduce tax and protect family wealth. We advise on conversion of a proprietorship or partnership into an LLP or company, family arrangements, partner exit, valuation and succession planning, along with the related income tax return filing and audit and assurance points. Every recommendation is explained in plain language, with both benefits and risks.

Business Advisory for Local Owners

We work with traders, small manufacturers, clinics, consultants, e-commerce sellers and first-generation founders in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar and Mira Bhayandar. A first meeting costs you nothing but time: bring your idea or your latest accounts, and we will tell you where you stand and what to do next. We can also connect advice with your GST registration and return filing and tax compliance so everything stays consistent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you help with MUDRA or bank loans?

Yes, we prepare project reports and supporting financials for loan applications. Approval remains the lender's decision.

Can you advise before I start a business?

Yes, early advice on structure and tax saves cost later.

What documents are needed for a project report?

Past financial statements, ITR and bank statements if available, quotations for machinery or stock, details of promoters, and the loan amount and purpose.

Do you help with Udyam registration?

Yes. We help you register and explain which benefits and schemes may apply to your business.

Can you advise on converting my proprietorship into an LLP or company?

Yes. We compare tax, compliance cost, liability and funding needs, and help with the conversion filings if you decide to go ahead.
