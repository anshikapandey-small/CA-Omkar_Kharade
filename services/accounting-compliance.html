Accounting, Bookkeeping & Compliance Services

Accurate books are the base of every tax return, loan and business decision. We provide bookkeeping, final accounts, payroll, MIS reporting and monthly compliance for traders, service providers and small companies in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar, Naigaon and nearby areas.

What We Handle

  • Daily or monthly bookkeeping in Tally or similar software
  • Bank and ledger reconciliation
  • Preparation of profit and loss account and balance sheet
  • Payroll, PF and ESI support
  • Monthly MIS and cash flow reports
  • Calendar of due dates for GST, TDS and income tax

Why Outsource Accounting?

A part-time accountant is often cheaper than a full-time hire, and a CA firm reviews the work for errors. Your books stay audit-ready, due dates are tracked for you, and you spend your time on sales instead of paperwork.

How We Work

You share bills and bank statements by email or WhatsApp, we record and reconcile them, and you receive a monthly summary with pending items clearly listed.

Bookkeeping Services for Small Businesses

Whether you run a shop, a clinic, a small manufacturing unit or a consulting practice, regular bookkeeping keeps you in control of cash and profit. We record sales, purchases, expenses, bank entries and journal entries, match them with your bank statements and GST returns, and give you ledgers that a banker or tax officer can read. Our accounting service in Virar and Vasai is available monthly, quarterly or on a project basis, and works on Tally or on the software you already use.

Final Accounts, Balance Sheet and MIS

At year end we prepare the profit and loss account, balance sheet and schedules, with notes where needed. These statements support your income tax return filing, loan applications and audit, if applicable. During the year we can send a simple monthly MIS showing sales, expenses, margins, receivables and payables, so you can act before a problem grows. For owners planning a loan, our business advisory team uses the same data to prepare project reports.

Payroll, PF, ESI and TDS Support

If you employ staff, we help with salary sheets, payslips, PF and ESI calculation where applicable, Professional Tax tracking and TDS on salary. We also help with TDS on payments to contractors, professionals and landlords, and with quarterly TDS returns. Correct TDS records protect your expense deductions and your vendors’ credit.

Books That Match Your GST and Tax Returns

Many notices arise because the books say one thing and the returns say another. We reconcile sales with GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B, purchases with GSTR-2B, and income with Form 26AS and AIS before returns are filed. This approach also keeps you ready if a audit and assurance is required. See our GST registration and return filing page for more.

What We Need From You

  • Sales and purchase bills, expense receipts and cash memos
  • Bank and credit card statements and loan statements
  • Previous year’s return, balance sheet and GST details
  • Payroll details if you have employees

You can send these by email or WhatsApp, and we confirm anything unclear before closing the month. Fees depend on the volume of entries and the scope of work, and we share a quote after a short discussion.

Serving Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar, Naigaon and Nearby Areas

Our office is in Virar (West), and we regularly work with clients in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar, Naigaon, Bhayandar, Mira Road, Boisar, Dahisar and the wider Mumbai western suburbs. Clients elsewhere in India are served online by email, WhatsApp and video call.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you work on Tally?

Yes, we can maintain books on Tally or work on the software you already use.

Can you handle only compliance and not bookkeeping?

Yes. Many clients keep their own books and use us for returns, TDS and reviews.

How much does bookkeeping cost for a small business?

It depends on the number of transactions, the software and the reports you need. We give a clear quote after reviewing a sample of your records.

Can you clean up old, unreconciled books?

Yes. We can rebuild and reconcile past records so that returns, loans or an audit can be completed. The time needed depends on how much is missing.

Do you provide monthly MIS reports?

Yes. A simple monthly report covers sales, expenses, profit, receivables, payables and pending compliance.
