Who Needs to File an Income Tax Return?

You must file a return if your total income crosses the basic exemption limit applicable to you, but many people file even when they are not required to. Filing is useful if you want to claim a refund, carry forward losses, apply for a loan or visa, or keep a clean financial record. Returns are also needed in cases such as holding foreign assets, high-value transactions or tax deducted above your liability. Because limits and conditions change with each Finance Act, we check the current rules for your situation before filing.

Old Tax Regime vs New Tax Regime

Every taxpayer must decide between the old and the new tax regime, and the right choice depends on your deductions. The old regime allows deductions such as Section 80C, health insurance under 80D, home loan interest and HRA. The new regime offers lower slab rates but fewer deductions. We run both calculations on your actual numbers so you choose the option that leaves more money with you, instead of guessing.

Documents Required for ITR Filing

  • PAN, Aadhaar and bank account details
  • Form 16 from your employer and Form 26AS / AIS
  • Interest certificates from banks and post office
  • Capital gains statements for shares and mutual funds
  • Rent receipts, home loan certificate and investment proofs
  • For business: books of accounts, bank statements and GST returns

Income Tax Notice? Do Not Ignore It

Receiving an income tax notice is common and does not always mean something is wrong. Notices are issued for reasons such as mismatch in TDS, unreported income, defective returns or selection for scrutiny. Each notice has a response deadline, so reply on time and with proper documents. Our team reads the notice, drafts the reply, files it on the income tax portal and represents you in assessment or appeal proceedings where needed.

TDS Compliance for Employers and Businesses

If you deduct tax at source on salary, rent, professional fees or contractor payments, you must deposit it and file quarterly TDS returns. Late filing attracts fees and interest, and wrong deduction can disallow expenses. We prepare TDS returns, issue Form 16 and 16A, correct defaults and help your vendors claim their credit.

Tax Planning: Save Tax Legally

Good tax planning starts before March, not after it. We review your income sources and suggest legal options such as Section 80C investments, health insurance under 80D, NPS contributions, home loan benefits and correct structuring of capital gains. For business owners we also look at depreciation, presumptive taxation and the choice between proprietorship, partnership, LLP and company. The aim is a plan you can follow all year, with documents ready when it is time to file.

Capital Gains, Freelancers and NRIs

Share, mutual fund and property transactions need careful reporting of purchase cost, holding period and exemptions. Freelancers and consultants must handle advance tax and presumptive income rules, while NRIs face special provisions on TDS and residential status. We prepare these returns with the supporting computations so every figure can be explained if questioned.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to file ITR?

The due date depends on your category, such as salaried individuals or audit cases, and it can be extended by the department. Contact us and we will confirm the current date for you.

Can I file ITR if I missed the due date?

Yes, you can usually file a belated return with applicable fees and interest, but some benefits such as carrying forward certain losses may be lost. File as early as possible.

Do I need a CA to file my income tax return?

It is not compulsory, but a CA helps you avoid mismatches, choose the right regime and respond to notices correctly.

How long does a refund take?

Once the return is e-verified and processed, refunds are generally credited to your pre-validated bank account. Timing varies, and we help track the status.
