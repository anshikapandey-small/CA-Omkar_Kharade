Company Registration & ROC Compliance Services

Choosing the right structure and staying compliant with the Registrar of Companies protects your business and your personal liability. We help with private limited company registration, LLP registration, partnership deeds and annual ROC filings for clients in Virar, Nalasopara, Vasai, Palghar, Naigaon and nearby areas.

Our Services

  • Private limited company and LLP incorporation
  • Partnership firm and proprietorship setup
  • Annual filings such as AOC-4 and MGT-7 for companies
  • Director KYC, appointment and resignation filings
  • Share transfer, capital changes and name or address changes
  • Company or LLP closure

Which Structure Suits You?

A proprietorship is simple, an LLP offers limited liability with lighter compliance, and a private limited company suits businesses planning to raise funds. We compare tax, compliance cost and liability for your case before you decide.

Why Annual Filing Matters

Missing ROC filings attracts daily additional fees, and directors can face disqualification for continued default. We keep a calendar and file on time.

Private Limited Company Registration

A private limited company needs at least two directors and two shareholders, and offers limited liability, a separate legal identity and easier access to funding. We guide you on name selection, director details (DIN), share capital, the registered office and the incorporation documents, and then file the incorporation on the MCA portal. After incorporation you also need a PAN, TAN, bank account and, where applicable, GST and other registrations. Ask us for a full checklist.

LLP Registration and Partnership Deeds

A Limited Liability Partnership needs at least two partners and offers limited liability with lighter compliance than a company. A well-drafted LLP agreement or partnership deed prevents disputes about profit sharing, capital, drawings and exit. We prepare the documents and file the registration where required. A One Person Company is another option for a sole founder who wants limited liability.

Annual ROC Filings for Companies and LLPs

Every company must hold its annual general meeting, file audited financial statements in Form AOC-4 and its annual return in Form MGT-7 or MGT-7A. Directors must complete DIR-3 KYC every year, and many companies also file an auditor appointment form. LLPs file Form 11 and Form 8 every year. Even a company with no business activity has to file. Our audit and assurance team supports the audit that comes before these filings.

Director Changes, Share Transfers and Closure

We handle appointment and resignation of directors, change of registered office, name change, increase in authorised capital, allotment and transfer of shares, and closure or strike-off of a company or LLP. Each of these needs the right resolutions, forms and timelines. Getting them right avoids disputes later.

What Happens If You Miss ROC Filings

Delays attract additional fees for every day of default, and long default can lead to director disqualification and the company being struck off. Reviving a struck-off company costs far more than filing on time, which is why we maintain a due-date calendar for each client. Related guidance appears in our Acts and Rules guide, forms guide and business advisory pages.

Documents Usually Needed for Incorporation

For each director or partner, we need a PAN, Aadhaar, passport size photograph, email address, mobile number and an address proof such as a bank statement or utility bill. For the registered office, we need proof of address, such as a rent agreement or sale deed, and a no objection letter from the owner. We then share a checklist with the names you want to propose.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does company registration take?

If documents are in order, incorporation usually takes a few working days, depending on government processing.

What documents are needed?

PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, photographs of directors and proof of the registered office.

Which is better for me: an LLP or a private limited company?

An LLP suits professionals and small businesses that want lighter compliance. A private limited company suits businesses planning to raise funds or issue shares. We compare both for your case.

Do I have to file ROC returns if my company has no business?

Yes. A company or LLP still has to file its annual forms, although the figures may be nil.

Can you help close a company or LLP I no longer use?

Yes. We help with the closure or strike-off process, including pending filings and the documents the Registrar requires.
