Who Needs GST Registration?

Registration is mandatory once your aggregate turnover crosses the threshold set for your type of business and state. It is also compulsory in specific cases regardless of turnover, such as inter-state supply of goods in many situations, supply through e-commerce platforms and certain notified categories. Many small businesses register voluntarily to claim input tax credit, work with larger buyers and build credibility. We review your business model and tell you whether registration is required right now.

Documents Required for GST Registration

  • PAN and Aadhaar of the proprietor, partners or directors
  • Photograph and contact details of the applicant
  • Proof of business address such as rent agreement and electricity bill, with owner consent
  • Bank account proof (cancelled cheque or statement)
  • Incorporation or partnership documents for firms and companies

GST Registration Process

We prepare the application on the GST portal, upload your documents and respond to any query raised by the officer. After verification you receive your GSTIN and registration certificate. We also set up invoicing formats and explain what you must charge, record and file from day one, so there are no surprises in your first return.

GST Return Filing: What You Must File

Regular taxpayers generally file GSTR-1 for outward supplies and GSTR-3B for summary tax payment, either monthly or under the quarterly scheme if eligible. Composition taxpayers follow a simpler return structure. Late filing attracts late fees and interest, and continuous non-filing can lead to registration cancellation. We prepare your returns from your sales and purchase data, compare them with your purchase register and file before the due date.

Input Tax Credit and Reconciliation

ITC is often the biggest saving for a business, and also the biggest area of dispute. You can claim credit only when the supplier has filed returns and the invoice reflects in your GSTR-2B, subject to conditions. We reconcile purchases with GSTR-2B every period, flag suppliers who have not filed and prevent excess or ineligible claims that can attract interest and penalty later.

GST Notices and Audit

GST officers issue notices for return mismatch, delayed filing, wrong ITC or turnover differences. A well-drafted reply with supporting documents resolves most notices at an early stage. We handle show-cause notices, assessments and appeals, and we also conduct GST audits to find errors before the department does.

GST for Startups, Traders and Service Providers

Each business type faces different GST questions. Traders need correct HSN codes and rate classification, service providers must decide place of supply, and exporters can claim refunds or export without payment of tax under the right conditions. Startups often need help choosing between regular and composition schemes. We guide you on rates, invoicing, reverse charge and record keeping so your books match your returns. We also explain how GST interacts with your income tax and TDS obligations, so that your sales figures stay consistent across every return you file during the year and no department can question a mismatch.

GST Cancellation, Amendment and Refund

If you close the business, change your address or add a new place of business, your registration must be updated. We file amendment and cancellation applications and also prepare refund claims for exports and inverted duty structures. Acting on time avoids notices and keeps your compliance rating clean.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does GST registration take?

If documents are in order, registration is usually completed within a few working days, though timing depends on the officer's verification.

What is the penalty for late GST return filing?

Late filing attracts a daily late fee and interest on unpaid tax. The amounts depend on the return type, so file on time or contact us to regularise.

Can I claim ITC if my supplier has not filed returns?

Generally no. The credit must reflect in your GSTR-2B, so we reconcile every period and follow up with non-compliant suppliers.

Is GST registration required for small service providers?

Only if your turnover crosses the applicable limit or a specific rule makes it mandatory. We check your case and advise.
